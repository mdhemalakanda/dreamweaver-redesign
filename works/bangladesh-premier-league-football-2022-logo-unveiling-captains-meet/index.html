Bangladesh Premier League Football 2022: Logo Unveiling & Captains’ Meet

Category
Sports
Client
Bangladesh Football Federation (BFF)

Client: Bangladesh Football Federation (BFF)

Event: TVS Bangladesh Premier League Football 2021–22

Event Type: Sports Event | League Opening | Logo Unveiling | Captains’ Meet

Date: 30 January 2022

Venue: Hotel Sheraton Banani, Dhaka, Bangladesh

Industry: Sports & Event Management

Agency: Imago

In 2022, Dreamweaver Events had the opportunity to deliver an important event connected with Bangladesh’s premier professional football competition — the TVS Bangladesh Premier League (BPL) Football 2021–22 Logo Unveiling & Captains’ Meet.

Held at Hotel Sheraton Banani, Dhaka, the event brought together senior officials of the Bangladesh Football Federation (BFF), representatives of the league’s partners and sponsors, football stakeholders, media and the captains of all 12 participating clubs. The official BFF record confirms that the programme was held on 30 January 2022, just days before the new BPL Football season was scheduled to begin on 3 February. (BFF)

Setting the Stage for Bangladesh’s Top Football League

The Bangladesh Premier League Football is one of the country’s most important domestic sporting competitions. The 2021–22 season featured 12 clubs and was organized under the Bangladesh Football Federation.

The 2022 logo unveiling was therefore more than a conventional corporate gathering. It was a season-opening sports event, designed to introduce the new BPL identity and bring together the clubs and their captains before the start of the competition.

Bangladesh Sangbad Sangstha (BSS) reported that the TVS Bangladesh Premier League Football logo was formally unveiled on 30 January 2022, with the league scheduled to start on 3 February. The news report also highlighted the presence of BFF President Kazi Mohammad Salahuddin, senior BFF officials, league stakeholders and representatives of the event’s commercial partners. (BSS)

The Asian Age also covered the event, reporting that the logo unveiling and captains’ meet brought together the captains of the 12 participating clubs. (The Asian Age)

Dreamweaver Events at BPL Football 2022

For Dreamweaver Events, being part of a national-level football event was an important milestone.

The event required a professional environment that could reflect the identity and importance of the Bangladesh Premier League. The stage, LED presentation, sound, lighting, event branding and overall production environment were designed to support the official programme and create a strong visual experience for guests, football officials, team representatives and the media.

Our work at the event reflected the importance of professional sports event production, where every element has to work together — from the stage and screen presentation to the audience experience and programme flow.

Bringing the BPL Captains Together

One of the central moments of the event was the Captains’ Meet, where representatives of all 12 participating clubs came together ahead of the new season.

According to contemporary BSS coverage, the participating club captains attended the ceremony and shared their expectations and targets for the upcoming league. (BSS)

The event also took place during a challenging period for sports and events. Bangladesh was experiencing a rise in COVID-19 cases, and BFF was taking additional precautions before starting the competition. BSS reported that players would require negative COVID-19 test results before being permitted to participate in the league. (BSS)

This added another layer of importance to professional event planning and execution at the time.

A Strong Sports Event Portfolio for Dreamweaver

For an event management company, experience with recognized sports properties demonstrates the ability to work in an environment where branding, production, timing, presentation and coordination are all critical.

The BPL Football 2022 Logo Unveiling & Captains’ Meet became part of Dreamweaver Events’ portfolio of professional event experiences — alongside corporate events, conferences, brand activations, exhibitions and other large-format productions.

The project also reflects our ability to create event environments for sports federations, professional leagues, sponsors, corporate stakeholders and media-facing programmes.
