Education — Signature Project

Shibaloy Government High School 75th Anniversary Celebration

Category
Education

Shibaloy Government High School celebrated 75 years of its proud educational journey with a grand day-long anniversary programme at the school grounds in Manikganj.

Established in 1949, the institution has played an important role in expanding education throughout the region. The historic celebration brought together nearly 4,000 former and current students, teachers, parents, distinguished guests and well-wishers.

Dreamweaver Events was proud to provide event decoration, lighting, artist management, design and creative services, and generator support for this memorable celebration.

Event Overview

The 75th anniversary celebration created a joyful reunion for generations of students and teachers. The programme began with a colourful rally and included flag hoisting, a formal discussion session, lunch, illumination and an entertaining cultural programme.

The event offered former students an opportunity to reconnect with their teachers and classmates, revisit cherished memories and celebrate the remarkable legacy of their beloved school.

Our Scope of Work

Dreamweaver Events supported the celebration with the following services:

Event decoration

Stage and venue lighting

Artist management and coordination

Event design and creative support

Generator and backup power support

On-site technical and operational coordination

Our team worked closely with the organising committee to create a festive environment while ensuring smooth coordination throughout the programme.

Cultural Programme and Artist Management

The cultural programme was one of the major attractions of the celebration. Renowned flutist Jalal Uddin, popular singers Safwan and Porshi, and the band Aces entertained the audience with memorable performances.

Dreamweaver Events handled artist management and coordination to help ensure that the performances were organised and delivered smoothly.

Creating a Memorable Celebration

An anniversary of this scale requires careful planning, creative presentation and dependable technical support. Our decoration and creative elements reflected the school’s proud 75-year heritage, while the lighting transformed the venue into a vibrant celebration space.

Reliable generator support ensured uninterrupted power for the lighting, performances and essential event operations.

It was an honour for Dreamweaver Events to contribute to this historic celebration. We sincerely thank the organising committee of Shibaloy Government High School for their trust and cooperation.
