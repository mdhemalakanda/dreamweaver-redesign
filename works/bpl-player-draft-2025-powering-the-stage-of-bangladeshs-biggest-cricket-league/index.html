Sports — Signature Project

“Bangladesh premier league” Player Draft 2025 – Powering the Stage of Bangladesh’s Biggest Cricket League

Category
Sports

For the third time, Dreamweaver Event proudly partnered with IMAGO Sports Management to bring to life one of the most anticipated events in Bangladesh’s cricket calendar — the Bangladesh Premier League (BPL) Player Draft.

Held at the elegant Pan Pacific Sonargaon Hotel, Dhaka, this edition marked the 11th season of the BPL, and once again, Dreamweaver was at the forefront of delivering world-class production, stage design, and technical execution.

Our Role: From Vision to Reality

As the official technical and creative support firm for IMAGO, Dreamweaver was responsible for transforming the venue into a grand stage worthy of the country’s premier cricket league.

Our contributions included:

3D Stage Design & Visualization – A modern, dynamic draft stage concept built to highlight the prestige of the BPL and create a high-impact visual identity.

Custom Woodwork – We built a sleek, premium-quality stage setup with detailed craftsmanship and functional design for the draft operations.

Professional Sound & Lighting – State-of-the-art sound systems and intelligent lighting created a vibrant and immersive atmosphere for both attendees and viewers.

LED Integration – High-resolution LED screens displayed live updates, player data, and visuals, enhancing the overall energy and flow of the event.

Draft Highlights

A total of 198 local players were listed, with 18 already signed by the seven participating franchises.

Big names like Najmul Hossain Shanto, Liton Das, Taskin Ahmed, Mahmudullah, and Rishad Hossain headlined the Category ‘A’ players.

For the first time in BPL history, a provision was introduced for an auction of unsold Category ‘A’ players.

439 overseas players were shortlisted for selection, with teams allowed to register an unlimited number of foreign players.

Strength in Partnership

Working once again with IMAGO Sports Management, one of Bangladesh’s most promising and innovative sports firms, we ensured that every technical detail met international standards. As their official technical support partner, Dreamweaver has consistently delivered excellence in all three editions of the BPL Draft we’ve collaborated on.
