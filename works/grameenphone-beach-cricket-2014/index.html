Sports — Signature Project

Grameenphone Beach Cricket 2014

Category
Sports

In 2014, Dreamweaver Events delivered the event management and production for Grameenphone Beach Cricket, a pioneering sports activation that brought four of Bangladesh’s top cricket stars together with their fans in an extraordinary beachside setting.

Designed as an immersive fan-engagement experience, the event transformed the beach into a fully operational cricket venue, combining professional sports production with entertainment, hospitality and audience engagement. Dreamweaver managed the complete event infrastructure and on-ground execution, ensuring a seamless experience for players, guests, performers and fans.

The Experience

The event was more than a cricket match—it was a unique sports and fan-engagement experience that connected Bangladesh’s cricket stars directly with their supporters. From preparing the beach cricket environment to coordinating hospitality, entertainment, technical production and photography, Dreamweaver handled the event from planning through execution.

Dreamweaver Events proudly delivered one of Bangladesh’s memorable beach-based sports activations, combining cricket, entertainment and fan engagement into one seamless experience.
