Concert & Live — Signature Project

Oshomvob Ke Somvob Kora Ananta’r Kaj (2013)

Category
Concert & Live

In 2013, Dreamweaver Events proudly managed the event production and execution of “Oshomvob Ke Somvob Kora Ananta’r Kaj”, a unique celebrity-fan engagement event organized by Live Technology at Radisson Blu Dhaka.

The event created an exclusive opportunity for selected fans to spend an intimate and memorable evening with two of Bangladesh’s most popular entertainment personalities, Ananta Jalil and Barsha. The carefully curated experience brought celebrities and their selected fans together in a premium hospitality environment, with an evening centered around interaction, entertainment and an exclusive dinner experience.

Dreamweaver Events was responsible for managing the event on the ground, coordinating the production, guest experience and operational requirements to ensure that every element of the evening was delivered smoothly and professionally.

Creating a Memorable Celebrity Experience

The evening was designed around a simple but powerful concept: bringing fans closer to the stars they admire. With Ananta Jalil and Barsha personally engaging with selected fans over dinner, the event created a warm, exclusive and memorable atmosphere.

Held at the prestigious Radisson Blu Dhaka, the event stood out as a distinctive celebrity engagement experience for its time in Bangladesh. Dreamweaver’s role was to bring together the venue, production, hospitality and guest experience into one seamless execution.

A Memorable Event in Bangladesh’s Entertainment Landscape

“Oshomvob Ke Somvob Kora Ananta’r Kaj” remains one of the distinctive events in Dreamweaver Events’ portfolio, demonstrating our ability to manage high-profile celebrity engagements, premium hospitality experiences and audience-focused events.

From planning and production to the final guest departure, Dreamweaver Events ensured that the experience was professionally managed and memorable for everyone involved.
