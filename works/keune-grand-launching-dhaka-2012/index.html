Corporate — Signature Project

Keune Grand Launching Dhaka (2012)

Category
Corporate

Dreamweaver Events proudly managed the Keune Grand Launching in Dhaka, marking a significant moment for the international beauty and professional haircare brand in Bangladesh.

Held at the prestigious Ruposhi Bangla Hotel, Dhaka, the grand launch brought together leading personalities from the beauty, fashion and lifestyle industries. The event was organized in collaboration with renowned choreographer JOJO, adding a strong creative and entertainment dimension to the launch experience.

A major highlight of the event was the presence of celebrated hairstylist and industry icon Javed Habib, who made his first appearance in Bangladesh for the occasion. His participation elevated the event into a major industry gathering and created strong excitement among professionals and audiences.

Creating an International Brand Experience

Dreamweaver Events transformed the venue into a polished brand environment that reflected the international positioning of Keune. From the stage and visual branding to sound and lighting, every production element was designed to create a sophisticated launch atmosphere.

The collaboration with JOJO and the arrival of Javed Habib in Bangladesh for the first time made the launch particularly memorable, bringing together international beauty, professional hairstyling, fashion and entertainment on one platform.

A Landmark Brand Launch

The Keune Grand Launching demonstrated Dreamweaver Events’ capability to deliver high-profile international brand launches with premium production standards, combining technical execution, creative presentation and celebrity-led experiences to create a memorable event for both the brand and its audience.
