Frequently Asked Questions

1. What services does Dreamweaver Events provide?

We provide event planning, stage production, sound, lighting, LED screens, branding, logistics and on-site coordination. You can choose complete event management or selected services.

2. What types of events do you manage?

We manage corporate conferences, seminars, AGMs, product launches, exhibitions, award ceremonies, factory openings and company anniversaries. We also support university programs, sports events, concerts and reunions.

3. Do you manage events outside Dhaka?

Yes. Based in Gulshan-2, Dhaka, we support events across Bangladesh. Transportation, accommodation and location-specific logistics are included in the quotation where required.

4. Can we hire only sound, lighting or LED screens?

Yes. You can book individual equipment services or a combined production package. We recommend a suitable setup based on your venue, audience size and program, with technical support specified in the quotation.

5. How much does event management cost?

Costs depend on your event size, venue, duration, production requirements and selected services. Share your requirements and budget range for an itemized quotation, or visit our Pricing Plan page for guidance.

6. How early should we contact you?

Contact us as soon as you have a proposed event date. Larger events require more time for planning, approvals and production. For urgent requests, we will confirm availability and what can be delivered within your timeline.

7. How can we request a quotation?

Call or WhatsApp +880 1730-828484, or visit our Contact Us page. Please share your event type, date, location, estimated guest count and required services so we can prepare a suitable proposal.
