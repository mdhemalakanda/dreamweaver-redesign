Category
Corporate

The GRID FORMING THE FUTURE – Sungrow Bangladesh Summit was an important corporate event that brought together event branding, product presentation, and a professionally prepared event environment. Organized by Mcom Shanghai, the summit required careful coordination of physical event elements, customized fabrication, branded display areas, and product presentation structures. Dreamweaver Events was proud to contribute to this occasion as the event’s logistics supporter, providing a range of services that helped bring the event environment together.

For this project, Dreamweaver Events delivered logistics and fabrication support with a focus on the event’s physical setup, branding requirements, and product presentation areas. Our responsibilities included logistics support, PVC work, branding structures, custom woodwork, acrylic work, product display setup, exhibition presentation structures, and on-site event support. Each element formed part of the overall event environment, helping ensure that the different areas were prepared and presented according to the event’s requirements.

The project reflects Dreamweaver Events’ involvement in corporate summit logistics, exhibition setup, event fabrication, and branded product presentation in Bangladesh. From preparing physical display components to assisting with the placement of customized structures, our team worked across different parts of the setup to support the event’s overall presentation.
