About Dreamweaver Events

Creating events people remember — since 2008.

Dreamweaver Events is a Bangladesh-based event management and event production company, established in 2008. For more than a decade, we have been bringing together creative ideas, professional planning, technical production and on-ground execution to create events that people remember. From corporate conferences and brand launches to sports events, university programs, reunions, concerts and social celebrations, we manage the details behind the experience—so our clients can focus on their guests, audience and objectives. We create events. People remember.

The Legacy of Dreamweaver Events

Our Heritage

Dreamweaver Bangladesh was founded on October 26, 2008, by the sons of the late Eng. M. A. Taher, inspired by the vision and guidance of their mother, Hasina Akhtar. Our journey is built on a family legacy deeply rooted in Bangladesh’s event and decoration industry. Our story traces back to TipTop Decorator, established in 1967, which became known for its innovative approach to event decoration and management in Dhaka during the years before 1971. That heritage continues to shape Dreamweaver today. From our early beginnings at Faridpur Stadium to producing contemporary corporate, social, entertainment and large-scale events, we have continued to evolve with the industry while remaining true to our core values of creativity, professionalism and dependable execution. Today, Dreamweaver Events is a full-service event management and event production company in Bangladesh, delivering memorable experiences for corporations, institutions, brands, universities and private clients. Whether you need a professional wedding planning service, a corporate conference, a brand activation, a sports event or a large-scale event management team in Bangladesh, we bring together experience, creativity and production expertise to turn your vision into reality. More than five decades of family heritage. More than a decade of Dreamweaver experience. One commitment—to create events people remember.
