Club Notredamian Gala Night 2025 | Sound & Light Support by Dreamweaver Events

The Club Notredamian, one of the most prestigious alumni clubs in Bangladesh, hosted its magnificent Gala Night on October 24 at the RAWOA Club, Dhaka. The evening was an unforgettable celebration of elegance, music, and nostalgia — a night where Notredamians from different generations reunited to relive their memories and celebrate unity.

As the official sound and light support firm, Dreamweaver Events had the privilege of providing complete technical support for this grand event. Known as one of the most trusted event support firms in Bangladesh, Dreamweaver once again ensured a flawless audio-visual experience that perfectly matched the prestige of Club Notredamian.

The night featured a stunning live performance by Konal, one of Bangladesh’s most talented singers, whose voice filled the ballroom with magic. The crowd swayed to her melodies, followed by a high-energy DJ session by DJ Himu, who closed the night with an electrifying performance that kept everyone on the dance floor till late. The sound system was professionally operated by Sajib, ensuring crystal-clear sound and perfect tonal balance throughout the evening.

For the event’s main sound system, Dreamweaver deployed a combination of ACT Pro Line Array and JBL SRX speakers, supported by Seer 218 Subwoofers to deliver deep, powerful bass that gave the performances a dynamic edge. On stage, musicians enjoyed professional-grade equipment including Yamaha Drums, JCM 2000 guitar amplifier, Hartke Bass amplifier, and Peavey KB 5 for keyboard sound.

The EON series monitors were a major highlight, providing crisp on-stage feedback that helped performers deliver their best. To maintain professional sound quality, Shure microphones and Sennheiser wireless microphones and in-ear systems were used — ensuring reliable performance and clarity across all vocal and instrumental channels.

Lighting was another key aspect of the event. Dreamweaver’s lighting setup transformed the RAWOA Club into a vibrant and elegant venue. From intelligent moving heads to colorful RGB washes and precise spotlights, every cue was timed perfectly to the rhythm of the music and the mood of the evening. The lighting design added depth, texture, and visual excitement to every performance, enhancing the atmosphere of this prestigious Club Notredamian event.

As a leading sound system and event support firm in Bangladesh, Dreamweaver Events once again proved its commitment to quality, professionalism, and perfection. With over 17 years of experience in the industry, Dreamweaver continues to provide world-class sound systems, lighting design, and stage setup for concerts, corporate shows, cultural nights, and alumni events.

The Club Notredamian Gala Night 2025 was not just another event — it was a showcase of technical excellence and musical brilliance, made memorable by Dreamweaver’s sound, lights, and expertise.
