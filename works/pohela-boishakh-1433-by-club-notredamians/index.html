Pohela Boishakh is one of the most emotionally powerful and culturally rich celebrations in Bangladesh. It represents a fresh beginning, unity, and the deep-rooted traditions that define our identity. In April 2026, as Bangladesh welcomed the Bengali New Year 1433, Dreamweaver Events proudly delivered full-scale technical production support for a prestigious celebration hosted by Club NotreDamians Bangladesh Limited.

This marked our 5th successful collaboration with the club—an achievement that reflects trust, consistency, and our commitment to excellence as one of the best event management firms in Bangladesh.

Our goal was simple yet powerful: 👉 Blend traditional Bengali culture with modern event technology 👉 Deliver seamless sound, lighting, and production 👉 Create an immersive and unforgettable guest experience

Our Role: Complete Technical Event Production

As the official Sound, Light, and Technical Support Partner, Dreamweaver Events handled the entire backend production. From planning to execution, our team ensured that every technical element worked flawlessly behind the scenes—so the audience could enjoy the celebration without distraction.

Our services included:

Professional sound system setup

Advanced lighting design and execution

Live mixing and audio control

Power distribution and load management

On-site technical team support

Safe rigging and cable management

This holistic approach is what makes us a top-rated event management company in Bangladesh, trusted by premium clients and organizations.

Professional Sound System Setup for Cultural Events

Sound is the heartbeat of any event—especially for cultural programs like Pohela Boishakh, where music plays a central role.

The Challenge

The venue had high ceilings and reflective glass surfaces, which can cause echo, distortion, and uneven sound distribution.

Our Solution

Dreamweaver Events designed a customized audio setup using industry-leading equipment:

Original JBL SRX 118 Subwoofers for deep, powerful bass

JBL EON Monitors for clear on-stage sound feedback

Hartke Bass Amplifier for live band performance

Behringer X32 Digital Mixer for precision audio control

Our sound engineers carefully tuned the system using digital equalization and real-time adjustments. The speaker placement was strategically optimized to ensure even sound coverage across the venue.

The Result

Crystal-clear vocals

Balanced instrument output

No echo or feedback issues

Consistent sound experience from front to back

This level of audio precision is why Dreamweaver Events is recognized as a leading event sound system provider in Bangladesh.

Creative Lighting Design for Pohela Boishakh

Lighting plays a crucial role in transforming a venue into a vibrant celebration space. For Pohela Boishakh, the visual identity revolves around red, white, and traditional motifs like Alpona and Mangal Shobhajatra.

Our Lighting Approach

We used a combination of:

Ambient Lighting to create a warm and welcoming atmosphere

Effect Lights to enhance stage energy during performances

LED PAR Lights for colorful stage washes

Spotlights to highlight performers and décor elements

Decor Enhancement

The venue was beautifully decorated with traditional elements such as:

Paper fans

Handmade kites

Cultural patterns

Our lighting team carefully positioned uplights and directional beams to highlight these textures, creating a 3D visual effect that elevated both the live experience and photography output.

The Result

Rich and vibrant color tones

Enhanced cultural aesthetics

Instagram-worthy visuals

Perfect lighting for video and photography

This is why clients choose Dreamweaver Events as a top lighting design company in Bangladesh.
