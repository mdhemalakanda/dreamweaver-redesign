Education — Signature Project

Honoring Roots – Dhaka University History Department Alumni Event 2023

Category
Education

At Dreamweaver Event, we believe in giving back to the places and people that shaped us. That’s why it was a moment of pride and gratitude for us to be part of the History Department Alumni Reunion 2023, held at the iconic TSC, Dhaka University.

Our Chief Operations Officer and Head of Accounts, Hasan Mahmood & Sadaf Afroz Jeeta, is a proud alumnus of the History Department at Dhaka University. It was during their university years that he began his journey as an entrepreneur in the events industry—deeply inspired by his beloved mentor, Mansoor Sir. Being a part of this meaningful event was not just a professional commitment, but a heartfelt homecoming.

Our Contribution

Dreamweaver was honored to handle the sound production and technical setup for the reunion. We provided our ACT PRO Line Array system along with JBL speakers to ensure exceptional sound quality throughout the event, supporting both performances and speeches with clarity and impact.

Musical Highlights

The celebration featured vibrant performances by current students, followed by unforgettable musical acts by two of Bangladesh’s most popular voices:

Dinat Jahan Munni

Sabbir Ahmed (Closeup 1)

Their soulful renditions and crowd-pleasing performances made the evening even more special, blending nostalgia with celebration.

A Heartfelt Celebration

This event was more than just a reunion—it was a celebration of legacy, learning, and lifelong connections. Dreamweaver is proud to have played a part in making it a memorable evening for all attendees, especially for those who, like Hasan Mahmood, carry the spirit of Dhaka University in their hearts.
