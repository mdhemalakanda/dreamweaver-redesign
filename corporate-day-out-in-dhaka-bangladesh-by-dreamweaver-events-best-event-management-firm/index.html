What is a Corporate Day Out?

A corporate day out is a planned recreational event where employees step away from their regular work environment to relax, connect, and recharge. It often includes outdoor activities, team-building games, entertainment, food, and travel arrangements—all handled professionally to ensure a stress-free experience.

Dreamweaver Events understands that every company has different goals. Whether it’s team bonding, celebrating success, or simply giving employees a refreshing break, we design customized corporate day out experiences that deliver real value.

—

Top Venues for Corporate Day Out in Dhaka

Dhaka and its surrounding areas offer excellent venues for corporate outings. Dreamweaver Events has long-term partnerships and strong business relationships with some of the most popular destinations.

1. Fantasy Kingdom

Located in Ashulia, this theme park is one of the most exciting venues for corporate day outs. With thrilling rides, water attractions, and open spaces, it’s perfect for both fun and relaxation.

2. Nandan Park

Also situated in Ashulia, Nandan Park is known for its beautiful environment and family-friendly attractions. Dreamweaver Events has been working with Nandan Park since its opening in 2008, giving us unmatched experience in organizing events at this venue.
