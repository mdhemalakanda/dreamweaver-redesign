Sports — Signature Project

Celebrating 25 Years of Excellence in Bangladesh Cricket

Category
Sports

Dreamweaver Events, recognized as the best event management firm in Bangladesh, successfully executed one of the best sports celebrations in Dhaka and nationwide — “25 Years in Test Cricket: Decentralization of the Game.”

This grand celebration, organized by the Bangladesh Cricket Board (BCB), celebrated the silver jubilee of Bangladesh Test cricket, making it one of the most memorable national sports events in Bangladesh. With large-scale production, advanced technical support, and creative execution, Imago sports management & Dreamweaver Events once again proved why it is considered a top event management company in Bangladesh.

Nationwide Sports Event Management in Bangladesh

This project stands out as one of the largest sports event management projects in Bangladesh, covering multiple divisions including Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Khulna, Rangpur, and Barishal.

The grand finale at Jibanananda Das Stadium, Barishal highlighted the future of cricket development in Bangladesh. Aminul Islam Bulbul, President of BCB, stated:

“We want to take cricket to a new height… decentralization of cricket is a must.”

This vision aligns with modern sports event planning in Bangladesh, focusing on:

Expanding cricket infrastructure across the country

Developing sports facilities in every division and district

Creating opportunities for grassroots players

Strengthening Bangladesh’s future in international cricket

Memorable Works by Dreamweaver Events & Imago Management

As part of the most memorable works by Dreamweaver Events and Imago Management, this event represents excellence in event production, event logistics, and technical execution in Bangladesh.
