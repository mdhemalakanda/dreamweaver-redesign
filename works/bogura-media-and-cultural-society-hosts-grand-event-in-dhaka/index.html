Bogura Media and Cultural Society Hosts Grand Event in Dhaka

Category
Corporate

On Feb 28, 2025, the Bogura Media and Cultural Society, Dhaka, organized a grand gathering in Banani, Dhaka, bringing together a vibrant audience to celebrate the rich cultural heritage of Bangladesh. To ensure the event’s success, we provided top-of-the-line JBL SRX speakers along with professional stage gear, ensuring clear, high-quality sound for the attendees.

Renowned musician and bass player Rashed, along with keys player Shohag, played a pivotal role as the event’s contact persons, ensuring smooth coordination. The event also featured a star-studded lineup of honorable singers, delivering memorable performances that captivated the audience. This collaboration highlighted the role of professional sound and event management in creating exceptional cultural experiences in Dhaka.
