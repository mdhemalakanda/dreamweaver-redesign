Category
Sports

The BSPA Sports Carnival 2025 Prize Giving Ceremony was a remarkable celebration of sportsmanship, achievement, and professional event execution. Designed and managed with precision, this event stands as a benchmark example of what the best sports event supporter in Dhaka can deliver.

Proudly sponsored by Cute, a brand known for its elegant identity and commitment to youth development in Bangladesh, the event reflected a perfect fusion of premium branding and impactful presentation.

Premium Stage & Branding Setup

Creating a visually stunning environment was one of the key priorities. High-quality wooden branding boards were used to deliver a clean, modern, and premium look across the venue. The stage, backdrop, and branding placements were strategically designed to ensure maximum visibility for guests, audiences, and media.

This level of detailed execution is what defines a top sports event management company in Dhaka.

World-Class Sound System for Sports Events

Audio quality is critical for any successful sports ceremony. For this event, we deployed industry-leading sound systems including:

JBL SRX (Mexican)

JBL EON 712

These powerful speakers ensured crystal-clear sound, strong coverage, and impactful delivery throughout the venue.

To maintain flawless sound control, the Behringer X32 digital mixer was used by our expert sound engineers. For speeches and hosting, the AKG Podium Microphone delivered sharp, noise-free audio — ensuring every announcement reached the audience perfectly.

If you’re searching for professional sound support for sports events in Dhaka, this setup represents industry standards.

Dynamic Lighting & Event Ambience

Lighting played a major role in enhancing the energy of the ceremony. Using RGB lighting solutions, we created a vibrant and engaging atmosphere that perfectly matched the spirit of a sports celebration.

From stage highlights to branding illumination, every lighting element was synchronized to deliver a visually dynamic experience.

Advanced Technical Support for Sports Events in Dhaka

At Dreamweaver Events, our strength lies in delivering complete and reliable technical support for sports events in Dhaka. We combine professional-grade equipment with an experienced technical team to ensure every event runs smoothly from start to finish.

Our sound system setup is designed to meet international standards. We use high-performance audio solutions like JBL SRX and JBL EON series speakers, ensuring powerful output, crystal-clear clarity, and full venue coverage. With advanced digital mixing consoles such as the Behringer X32, our engineers maintain perfect balance for speeches, announcements, and live performances — making us a trusted choice for sports event sound support in Bangladesh.
