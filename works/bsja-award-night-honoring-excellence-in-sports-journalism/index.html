BSJA Award Night – Honoring Excellence in Sports Journalism

Dreamweaver Event proudly served as the official logistics partner for the prestigious BSJA Award Night, organized by TEM Sports in collaboration with the Bangladesh Sports Journalists Association (BSJA)—one of the most respected and long-standing journalist associations in Bangladesh.

Held at the Mirpur Indoor Stadium, this high-profile event celebrated the achievements of the country’s top sports journalists, athletes, and contributors to Bangladesh’s sporting community.

Our Role – Precision, Power & Production

As the logistics partner, Dreamweaver ensured flawless technical execution, delivering a world-class audiovisual experience worthy of the occasion.

Key Highlights of Our Work:

Sound Reinforcement:

ACT PRO Line Array for crystal-clear sound dispersion throughout the stadium

JBL SRX and Peavey QW4 systems for powerful and immersive Front of House (FOH) sound

Stage & Lighting Setup: Precision rigging, stage design, and professional-grade lighting systems to create a vibrant and dignified atmosphere

Artist & Guest Coordination: On-site logistics and technical support for performers, speakers, and award presenters

A Night to Remember

From live sports coverage to in-depth storytelling, the BSJA has been the voice behind Bangladesh’s sporting heroes. We were honored to contribute to an event that recognized these voices. With our deep expertise in event logistics and technical production, Dreamweaver helped make the night not just successful, but truly memorable.
