Bashundhara ’85 Reunion 2026 – Professional Event Sound Production by Dreamweaver Events

Category
Community

Dreamweaver Events provided professional event sound production and live audio engineering for the Bashundhara ’85 Reunion – Bristidharay Bondhumela 2026, a special gathering bringing together alumni, guests, artists and friends for a day of celebration, interaction and live musical performances.

For an event combining speeches, presentations, audience interaction and live band performances, reliable sound reinforcement was essential. Dreamweaver Events designed and operated a professional audio setup focused on clear speech, balanced music reproduction, consistent audience coverage and dependable live performance monitoring.

The production featured professional audio equipment including JBL SRX 725 front-of-house speakers, JBL EON stage monitors and a Behringer X32 digital mixing console, supported by an experienced technical team.

Event Overview

The Bashundhara ’85 Reunion 2026 was designed as a celebration of friendship, memories and music. The program included alumni interactions, speeches, entertainment and live musical performances.

Dreamweaver Events was responsible for the professional audio production required to support the different segments of the program.

Event Production Requirements

The sound system needed to deliver:

Clear and intelligible speech for hosts and speakers

Consistent sound coverage throughout the venue

Powerful and balanced audio for live music

Dedicated stage monitoring for performers

Flexible mixing for multiple musical instruments

Reliable transitions between speeches, presentations and performances

Professional live sound engineering throughout the event

Professional Sound System for the Reunion

Dreamweaver Events selected and configured the audio system according to the requirements of the venue and program.

The production setup included JBL SRX 725 loudspeakers for the main audience sound, JBL EON powered monitors for performers and a Behringer X32 digital mixing console for live audio control.

The combination allowed the technical team to maintain clarity during spoken segments while providing the power and control required for the live band performance.

JBL SRX 725 Front-of-House Sound System

The main audience sound system used JBL SRX 725 dual 15-inch loudspeakers as the front-of-house solution.

The system was selected to provide strong output, detailed midrange reproduction and clear vocal performance across the audience area. Proper speaker positioning, system tuning and level management were important to achieving consistent sound while avoiding unnecessary distortion or excessive volume.

For a reunion program that moved between speeches and live music, the system provided the flexibility required for both spoken-word and musical content.

JBL EON Stage Monitors for Live Performers

Live musicians need to hear their vocals and instruments clearly on stage. Dreamweaver Events used JBL EON powered speakers as stage monitors to provide performers with dedicated monitoring.

Proper stage monitoring helps musicians maintain timing, pitch and performance confidence while reducing the need for excessive stage volume.

The monitoring setup supported vocalists and instrumentalists during the live musical segment of the reunion.

Behringer X32 Digital Mixing Console

At the centre of the audio production was a Behringer X32 digital mixing console.

The X32 provided the technical team with the flexibility to manage multiple microphones and instruments, create dedicated monitor mixes and make real-time adjustments during the program.

During the live band performance, individual sources such as vocals, guitars, keyboards and percussion could be balanced and processed independently to create a controlled and natural live mix.

Live Band Sound Engineering

The musical segment featured a combination of vocal and instrumental performances, including acoustic and electric guitars, keyboards, percussion and vocals.

Live band production requires considerably more than simply connecting microphones to speakers. Each instrument needs to be properly gain-staged, equalized and balanced so that the individual elements remain clear without competing for the same frequency space.

Dreamweaver’s sound engineers managed the live mix throughout the performance, maintaining a balanced relationship between vocals, instruments and overall system output.

Why Professional Sound Matters at Alumni Reunions

An alumni reunion is more than a social gathering. It is an experience built around conversations, memories, speeches, performances and shared moments.

Poor sound can make speeches difficult to understand and can significantly reduce the impact of live entertainment. Professional event sound production ensures that guests can hear the program clearly while performers receive the monitoring they need to perform confidently.

For Bashundhara ’85 Bristidharay Bondhumela 2026, Dreamweaver Events focused on combining professional equipment, technical preparation and experienced live sound engineering to create a dependable audio experience.
